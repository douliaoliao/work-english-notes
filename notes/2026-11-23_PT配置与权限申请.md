# 2026-11-23｜PT 配置与权限申请

### 中文原意（根据本次对话整理）

今天处理 PT 配置时，我发现自己没有 PT 生产环境的访问权限，因此向 IT Support 提交了申请权限的工单。这个问题可能影响测试，并进一步影响 PT 部署，因为发布需要等待配置测试完成。我会在站会后跟进工单，收到 IT 回复后在 Teams 群聊中同步。如果今天仍没有回复，我会在明天的站会上再次提醒大家。

### 最终英文

Today, I worked on the PT configuration, but I realized I didn't have access to the PT production environment, so I submitted a ticket to IT Support to request access. This might affect testing and delay the PT deployment, because we need to finish testing the configuration before we can release. After the stand-up, I'll follow up on the ticket. Once IT replies, I'll update the team in the Teams chat. If there's still no reply by tomorrow's stand-up, I'll bring it up again then.

### 同事追问与推荐回答

**Q: Does that impact today's deployment, or just PT testing?**

中文：这会影响今天的部署，还是只影响 PT 测试？

**A:** It might affect the PT deployment as well, because the release depends on completing the PT configuration testing.

中文：也可能影响 PT 部署，因为发布依赖于 PT 配置测试的完成。

**Q: When will you update the team?**

中文：你什么时候给团队更新进展？

**A:** I'll follow up on the ticket after the stand-up and update the team in the Teams chat once IT replies.

中文：我会在站会后跟进工单，收到 IT 回复后在 Teams 群聊中同步。

**Q: What will you do if IT doesn't reply today?**

中文：如果 IT 今天没有回复，你会怎么做？

**A:** If there's still no reply by tomorrow's stand-up, I'll bring it up again then.

中文：如果到明天站会时仍没有回复，我会在会上再提一下。

### 重点表达

| 中文 | 英文 | 用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 依赖于 | depends on | 动词：The release depends on testing. |
| 对……有依赖 | has a dependency on | 名词结构：The release has a dependency on testing. |
| 跟进工单 | follow up on the ticket | 将来计划：I'll follow up on the ticket. |
| 申请权限 | request access | I submitted a ticket to request access. |
| 等测试完成 | wait until testing is complete | We need to wait until testing is complete. |
| 再次提起这个问题 | bring it up again | I'll bring it up again at tomorrow's stand-up. |
